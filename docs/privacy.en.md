## 2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the product or service used, we may collect:

* Full name.
* Contact information, such as email address, telephone number, and address.
* Information necessary to create and manage an account.
* Information related to companies, organizations, or projects.
* Technical information about the device and browser.

* Information necessary to process payments or provide contracted services, where applicable.

HyperCloud will seek to collect only the information necessary for the corresponding purposes.

## 3. Purposes of Processing

Personal data may be processed for:

* Creating, managing, and maintaining user accounts.
* Providing, operating, and improving our products and services.
* Providing technical support and customer service.
* Processing requests, contracts, payments, and transactions.
* Maintaining the security of our platforms and infrastructure.
* Preventing fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, and unlawful activities.
* Performing diagnostics, technical analysis, and maintenance.
* Sending communications related to contracted services.
* Informing users about updates, changes, new features, or services, when authorization or another lawful basis permits it.
* Complying with legal, contractual, or regulatory obligations.
* Exercising and defending HyperCloud's rights.
* Developing and improving our technology products.

When applicable law requires prior authorization for a specific purpose, HyperCloud will request such authorization.

## 4. Processing of Children's Data

Our services are not deliberately directed at minors when their use requires authorization from their legal representatives.

When the processing of personal information belonging to children or adolescents is applicable, HyperCloud will apply the safeguards and conditions established by Colombian law and will give primary consideration to the protection of their fundamental rights.

## 5. Authorization for Processing

When legally required, we will request the data subject's prior, express, and informed authorization for the processing of their personal data.

Authorization may be provided through physical, electronic, or technological mechanisms that allow HyperCloud to subsequently demonstrate that the data subject was informed and accepted the corresponding processing.

The data subject may withdraw their authorization when legally permitted. Withdrawal will not affect processing lawfully carried out prior to the withdrawal.

## 6. Information Security

HyperCloud implements reasonable technical, human, administrative, and organizational measures designed to protect personal information against loss, destruction, unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or misuse.

These measures may include authentication mechanisms, access controls, encryption, activity logs, infrastructure monitoring, backups, and security procedures.

However, no system connected to the Internet can guarantee absolute security. Therefore, HyperCloud cannot guarantee that information will be completely free from any security risk.

## 7. Providers and Third Parties

To provide certain services, we may use technology providers, infrastructure operators, hosting services, payment processors, communications services, analytics services, security services, or other specialized tools.

When such third parties have access to personal data on behalf of HyperCloud, we will seek to establish the corresponding contractual obligations and security measures in accordance with applicable law.

HyperCloud will not sell the personal data of data subjects.

## 8. International Transfers and Transmissions

Some technology providers used by HyperCloud may operate infrastructure or process information from countries other than Colombia.

When an international transfer or transmission of personal data occurs, HyperCloud will apply the conditions and safeguards required by Colombian law and will seek to use providers that offer appropriate levels of protection and security.
